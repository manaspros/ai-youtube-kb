## Key ideas
- **VibeThinker: a 3B MIT-licensed reasoning model reports math and competitive-coding scores near models 200x its size, but the claims are unverified and the authors say it is a specialist.** — Scores: AIME 2026 94.3, HMMT 2025 89.3, BRUMO 2025 93.8, LiveCodeBench v6 80.2, compared to DeepSeek V3.2 (671B) and Kimi K2.5 (>1T). The model was not trained on tool-calling or agent data and the authors advise against using it for coding agents or API orchestration. Critics raised possible training-data leakage and missing standard benchmarks such as a deep-SWE benchmark. ([1:20](https://youtu.be/aGseXhwqiLg?t=80))
- **Midjourney announced a ring-array ultrasound ('ultrasonic CT') body scanner, but it is far from a product: pre-FDA, with its first site not due until late 2027.** — The subject descends through a ring of about half a million sensors that act as both speaker and microphone, at roughly 5 cm/s, with the whole process targeted at under 60 seconds. Reconstruction works by analysing how wave shape changes across tissues. Raw data is terabytes per second. Initial focus is body-composition maps, with plans to submit test results to the FDA over time. The stated 2031 goal is 50,000+ scanners. ([3:58](https://youtu.be/aGseXhwqiLg?t=238))
- **Alibaba's Qwen Robot Suite splits the robotics pipeline into navigation, manipulation and world modelling, and only two of the three are released.** — Qwen Robot Nav is built on Qwen3-VL and unifies five navigation tasks, scoring 76.5% on VLN. Qwen Robot Manip uses a unified state-action space so one model covers robots with different arm geometries, trained on about 38,100 hours of mixed robot, human and synthetic video, and ranks first on RoboChallenge Table30 v1. Qwen Robot World uses natural language as the action interface; it is paper-only so far. ([7:46](https://youtu.be/aGseXhwqiLg?t=466))
- **DreamX World generates explorable worlds in real time, with a 'world memory' that retrieves earlier generations when the camera revisits a location.** — Training is staged: first basic dynamics and fine-grained camera control, then open-ended event response, then reinforcement learning for action-following and visual consistency. The model is about 5B parameters, with code on GitHub, weights on Hugging Face and a paper on arXiv. ([10:08](https://youtu.be/aGseXhwqiLg?t=608))
- **Higgs Audio V3 is a conversational TTS model with inline emotion, style and sound-effect tokens, but its license is research and non-commercial only.** — The authors report single-digit WER on 100 of 111 languages on their internal benchmark, plus top or near-top results on public voice-cloning benchmarks and on an emotion-focused benchmark. Inline tokens cover more than 21 emotions, singing, shouting, whispering and SFX. ([12:42](https://youtu.be/aGseXhwqiLg?t=762))
- **Molmo Motion predicts how marked 3D points on an object will move over the next few seconds, from one image plus a text instruction.** — It comes in two decoders: an autoregressive one that is smoother when the future path is clear, and a flow-matching one that handles multiple plausible outcomes. The paired PointMotionBench has 2,700 human-validated clips across 111 categories, and the model beats pixel-space video generators, parametric 3D methods and constant-velocity extrapolation on it. Weights, a training set of over a million videos and the benchmark are all released. ([13:54](https://youtu.be/aGseXhwqiLg?t=834))
- **Apodex One's deep research design is an orchestrator that spawns parallel sub-agents, plus a separate global verifier that audits all gathered evidence before the final answer.** — In deployment it coordinates up to 150 sub-agents over 15,000 steps per task. The host says that turning on heavy-duty team mode lifts the base model by 14.8 points on BrowseComp and 18.4 points on Frontier Science Research, which is evidence that the architecture contributes separately from the model. A 4B open model is reported to beat every open 30B-class model on BrowseComp. ([16:16](https://youtu.be/aGseXhwqiLg?t=976))
- **Happy Oyster (Alibaba) is a hosted real-time world engine that accepts commands mid-generation, and OmniDirector (Kling team) clones camera moves onto new images via a camera grid representation.** — Happy Oyster has a Directing mode (up to 3 min at 720p, controlling camera and storyline) and a Wandering mode (first-person, up to 1 min at 480p) with synchronized audio. It is not open source and was previously named Happy Horse 1.0. OmniDirector renders camera motion through empty 3D space as a grid and injects it as a control signal. It supports multi-shot sequences and its code is on GitHub. ([18:39](https://youtu.be/aGseXhwqiLg?t=1119))

## Techniques
- **Orchestrator, parallel sub-agents, and a global verifier** — A lead agent decomposes a research task and spawns specialised sub-agents that explore different angles in parallel. A separate verifier agent audits all collected evidence before the final answer is committed. Heavy-team mode was credited with large gains over the same base model (per the video, self-reported). *When:* Long-horizon deep research or any multi-step task where evidence quality matters more than a single agent's loop length.
- **Progressive training for interactive world models** — Stage 1 teaches basic world dynamics and fine-grained camera control; stage 2 teaches response to open-ended events; stage 3 applies RL to improve action-following and visual consistency. *When:* Building controllable generators where consistency under user actions matters more than a single clip's quality.
- **World memory via retrieval of prior generations** — When the camera revisits a location already generated, the model retrieves the earlier output for that location, so the scene stays spatially consistent rather than being regenerated. *When:* Explorable or navigable generated environments, to avoid inconsistent rooms on revisit.
- **Camera grid as a control signal** — Render the desired camera trajectory through empty 3D space into a grid representation, then inject that motion data into the video generation model alongside other conditioning signals. *When:* Transferring a reference camera move onto a different source image or shot.
- **Two decoders for trajectory prediction** — Predict trajectories autoregressively step by step when the future path is clear, or generate the whole trajectory directly in continuous space with flow matching when several outcomes are plausible. *When:* Predicting future object or point motion for robotics or video generation, depending on whether the future is unimodal.
- **Unified state-action space for cross-embodiment control** — Map robots with different arm lengths and joint layouts into one shared state-action representation, so one manipulation model can control several embodiments without per-robot training. *When:* Training a single policy across heterogeneous robot hardware.
- **Inline control tokens in TTS input** — Emotion, style (singing, whispering, shouting) and sound-effect cues are embedded as inline tokens in the text, so the script itself controls delivery. *When:* Expressive conversational voice agents where per-utterance prosody must be controlled from text.
