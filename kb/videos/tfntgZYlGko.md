## Techniques
- **Suno custom-mode song generation** — 1) Log in with any offered sign-in option. 2) Open the Create tab and turn on Custom Mode. 3) Get lyrics either from ChatGPT (paste them in) or by typing a topic so Suno writes them. 4) Enter the music style. Suno pre-fills a title, which can be kept or changed. 5) Click Create and pick the better of the two returned variants. *When:* Making a full song with lyrics for casual or hobby use, with no setup beyond the web UI.
- **Instrumental track generation** — In Custom Mode, turn on the instrumental toggle, enter a style, enter a title, and click Create. Lyrics are not needed. *When:* Background music, soundtracks or beats where vocals are not wanted.
- **Steering with the style and genre list** — Open Suno's style and genre list and pick the genre or descriptors that match the target sound before writing the style field. *When:* The first generation does not sound like what you wanted and you need a more specific style description.
