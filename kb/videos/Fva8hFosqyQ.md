> swyx's keynote "The Highest Loop" argues that core AI engineering skill is choosing which feedback loop you work in, moving up for productivity and scaling, down for reliability, and that the conference itself is the loop that makes loops.

## Summary
swyx opens the live stream with a metaphor: AI work has moved from tokens to chat to tools to goals to automations (cron jobs and loops). He says a core skill for AI engineers is knowing which loop they are in, going up a loop to scale productivity and inference, and going down to fix reliability bugs. He extends the idea to a human loop, from heartbeat to talking to tools to working in tiny teams and wider human projects. He notes that agents working in one domain are generalizing and that the conference is adding vertical tracks (healthcare, finance, GTM). He gives attendance history (about 2,000 in 2024, about 6,000 targeted and about 7,000 reached in 2025) and urges early ticket purchase. He closes by framing the conference and its summits as "the highest loop": humans gathering to decide the next loop. The talk is a keynote with little technical content.

## Key ideas
- **Core AI engineering skill is choosing which loop you are working in** — Going up a loop targets productivity and scaling (for example inference). Going down targets reliability and bug fixing. The speaker calls this a stack of nested loops and says engineers, leaders and founders should recurse across them. ([1:31](https://youtu.be/Fva8hFosqyQ?t=91))
- **Progression of AI work toward automation** — The arc runs token, then chat, then tools, then goals, then automations such as cron jobs and loops. Loop stacking is the current theme. ([0:58](https://youtu.be/Fva8hFosqyQ?t=58))
- **Human loop analogy** — Human development is framed as a loop: heartbeat, talking, tool use, working, then teams and families. The speaker says tiny teams matter more over time and that the same logic extends to countries, companies and civilization. ([3:08](https://youtu.be/Fva8hFosqyQ?t=188))
- **Agents are generalizing beyond coding** — Agents that work well in one domain are expected to generalize. The conference is adding vertical tracks such as healthcare, finance and GTM, which the speaker reads as AI diffusing beyond coding. ([4:13](https://youtu.be/Fva8hFosqyQ?t=253))
- **The highest loop is humans deciding the next loop** — The speaker argues the top loop is where people meet to decide what comes next. This is his reason for running conferences and for organizing AI Engineer summits in several cities. ([5:56](https://youtu.be/Fva8hFosqyQ?t=356))

## Techniques
- **Loop-level routing** — Decide which loop you are in. Move up a loop to find productivity and scaling gains. Move down a loop to find reliability issues and bugs. *When:* When planning AI engineering work, choosing where to spend effort between scaling a system and hardening it.
