## Techniques
- **Generate a deck from pasted notes in Gamma** — Sign in with Google, click Create New, choose Paste in Text, paste the notes, pick Presentation (vs. website or document), click Continue, set content amount to Brief, Medium, or Detail, keep English or pick one of 20+ languages, choose internet images or AI-generated images (default model Flux), set the card count, pick a design, and generate. *When:* Turning rough notes or an outline into a first-draft slide deck quickly, then editing, instead of formatting by hand.
- **Replace a generated image** — Click the existing image, remove it, then upload your own, search the web for one, or generate a new one with AI (costs credits). *When:* The generated image is off-topic or misplaced and you want a specific asset.
- **Reorganize a card's information layout** — Click the card's organization control and choose a format from the menu. The narrator selects the timeline format for a chronological card. *When:* Content is sequential or chronological and a bullet list reads poorly.
- **Keep AI-generation cost in check** — Prefer web or uploaded images over AI-generated ones, since each AI image spends credits. Keep card count within the free 10-card limit when possible. *When:* Working on the free tier or with a limited credit budget.
