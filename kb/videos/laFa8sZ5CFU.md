## Techniques
- **Multi-expert RL distillation into one motion-tracking policy (HumanoidGPT)** — Train specialized RL motion experts on different motion families, then distill their behavior into a single transformer generalist. Scale the training corpus by merging public mocap datasets with proprietary recordings (2B frames claimed). *When:* When one policy must track many motion styles zero-shot without per-motion training, and single-policy agility and generalization trade off against each other.
- **Lightweight fine-tuning for out-of-distribution motions** — Take the generalist tracker and run only a few steps of light fine-tuning on a new motion to reach behavior far outside the original training data (the presenter cites a 360-degree spin kick and a backflip). *When:* When a new motion must be learned quickly on top of a large pretrained policy instead of retraining from scratch.
- **VR teleoperation demonstrations as training data (Atlas)** — A human wears a VR headset and guides the robot through a task several times, move by move; the recorded trajectories train the robot's AI model to perform the task autonomously. *When:* When hand-coding behaviors is impractical and you can collect expert demonstrations on the real hardware.
- **Fleet telemetry feeding model training (Figure Helix)** — Each deployed robot generates real-world data that is folded back into the humanoid AI model, which the company credits with new terrain-aware whole-body control. *When:* When a deployed fleet can generate enough varied real-world data to improve the policy faster than simulation alone.
- **End-of-line functional test gating with burn-in (Figure BotQ)** — Each robot passes more than 80 functional verification tests before sign-off, including multi-limb stress tests and burn-in sessions of thousands of repeated full-body motions (squats, shoulder presses) to surface early failures. *When:* When hardware failures are expensive after deployment and yield has to be improved at production scale.
- **Visible intent display for robots working near people (Eno cognitive interface)** — Show the robot's planned next action and reasoning on an on-board screen so people nearby can anticipate its movement. *When:* When a robot shares a workspace with humans and predictability affects safety or trust.
