## Summary
The video compares the $20/month ChatGPT Plus and Claude Pro plans feature by feature. ChatGPT Plus gets GPT-5 modes, legacy models, Sora video, voice mode, image generation, custom GPTs and Codex. Claude Pro gets its model selector, a 200K context window, Claude Code, MCP connector support and research without a separate quota. The usage limits are the central comparison: ChatGPT Plus uses a 3-hour window (80 standard GPT-5 messages), while Claude Pro uses a 5-hour window (45 messages) and then blocks until it resets. Context is 32K for ChatGPT Plus versus 200K for Claude. File upload rules differ: ChatGPT allows larger files within a per-window cap, while Claude caps uploads per chat but has unlimited project uploads bounded by context. The speaker concludes that ChatGPT suits ideation and creative work, Claude suits long-form content and coding, and the choice depends on the user's use case. Neither plan includes API access. The video is consumer-level, its limit numbers are a snapshot from December 2025, and a sponsored Outskill segment sits in the middle.

## Techniques
- **Put large reference sets in a Claude project** — Upload documents to a Claude project rather than per chat. The speaker says project uploads are unlimited and bounded only by the context window. Per-chat uploads are capped at about 20 files of about 30MB each. *When:* Ongoing work that references many large PDFs or research files over multiple sessions.
- **Run Claude deep research for a cited report** — Turn on research mode in Claude and give it the topic. Answer its clarifying questions, then wait 20 minutes or more. The output is a long document with inline citations and links. *When:* A broad literature or web sweep where the output needs citations and source links, and where tool-usage limits are acceptable.
