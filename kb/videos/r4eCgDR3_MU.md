## Summary
The host (AI LABS channel) walks through the Kling AI web app, which has AI Images, AI Video and an AI Video Editor marked as coming soon. Sign-up is by email and password and gives free credits, with 66 free credits per day stated. For video, the settings are creativity level, mode, video length, aspect ratio, camera movement (the host uses pan and vertical) and an optional negative prompt. The host generates a camel-in-desert pan clip, which takes about 4 to 5 minutes and which they say has visible leg and step artifacts, and a dog-surfing clip with a vertical camera move, which they say has no noticeable flaws. The image generator takes a prompt, an optional reference image, an aspect ratio and an image count, and the host praises the detail of the 2-image and 4-image outputs. Image-to-video was not working at recording time, and free credits run out after about six videos a day, after which a paid plan is needed. The video has no benchmarks, comparisons or engineering detail, and the speaker's praise is not backed by evidence beyond their own samples.

## Techniques
- **Generate a text-to-video clip in Kling's web app** — Sign up with email and password to get free credits. Open the AI Video section from the home page. Enter a prompt, which the host says is in the video description. Set creativity, mode, length and aspect ratio, pick a camera movement (for example pan or vertical), optionally add a negative prompt, then click Generate. Wait about 4 to 5 minutes. *When:* When you want a quick, no-code look at a text-to-video model's output and prompt adherence. The video does not show prompt-engineering guidance beyond these steps.
- **Generate images with reference and count settings** — Open the AI Image section from the home page. Enter a prompt, optionally attach a reference image, set the aspect ratio and the number of images (2 or 4 in the video), then click Generate. *When:* When you want quick image variants from one prompt, or to test whether a reference image steers the output.
